🧑‍💼 CAREERS

Join a Team Built on Trust, Discipline, and Excellence

Northwinds is more than a risk management firm — we are a standard-bearer for professionalism, integrity, and quiet authority in Kenya’s security and compliance sector. We recruit people who value discretion, skill, and service above visibility. If you take pride in doing excellent work that speaks for itself, we invite you to explore a future with us.

🤝 WHY WORK WITH NORTHWINDS?

  • ✅ Professional Growth — Continuous training, certification, and clear career pathways
  • ✅ Fair & Competitive Terms — Industry-compliant pay, structured benefits, and timely remuneration
  • ✅ Respect & Dignity — Zero favouritism, transparent processes, equal opportunity
  • ✅ Meaningful Work — Protecting people, brands, and critical operations across Kenya
  • ✅ Discreet Culture — We value competence over profile; your work matters more than your public image
  • ✅ PSRA-Aligned — Fully licensed, compliant, and professionally recognised

🔍 WHO WE LOOK FOR

We do not require flashy credentials — we require character, reliability, and ability.

  • High integrity — honesty in every situation
  • Discretion — ability to keep sensitive information confidential
  • Physical & mental discipline — alert, calm under pressure
  • Team orientation — communicates clearly, follows protocol, supports colleagues
  • Kenyan citizen — valid national ID, clean record
  • Relevant experience an advantage but attitude and willingness to learn come first

We are an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all.

  • Requirements: Research skills, observant, good report writing

And many more roles — we welcome expressions of interest from qualified candidates.

📝 HOW TO APPLY

Send your CV, cover letter, and copies of certificates to:

📧careers@northwinds.co.ke

📍 Subject line: Position Name – Your Full Name

If no specific role is listed, you may send a general application with the subject: General Application – [Your Name]

✅ What to Include

  • Full name, phone number, email, location
  • Education & professional qualifications
  • Relevant work experience — be clear and honest
  • Any licenses or special training (PSRA, driving, first aid, etc.)
  • Brief note on why you want to join Northwinds

⚠️ IMPORTANT

  • We do not charge any fee at any stage of recruitment
  • No agents — all applications go directly to the email above
  • Shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interview within 14 days
  • Only shortlisted applicants will receive a response
